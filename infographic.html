How AI Became Normal

Technologies do not become part of everyday life all at once. They spread because they become useful, accessible, convenient, and increasingly difficult to imagine life without.

Question: What happens when a technology stops feeling like technology and starts feeling like an ordinary part of life?

The Bigger Question

The spread of AI raises a question that goes beyond whether the technology is useful. As technologies become naturalized, people can change their habits, expectations, and relationships around them.

This is the idea of co-constitution: humans shape technology, but technology can also shape human behavior and the environments in which we live.

Think about it If AI becomes easier, faster, and more useful every year, where should humans draw the line between using a tool and depending on it?
